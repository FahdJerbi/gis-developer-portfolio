GIS AUTOMATION & ETL 2023 – 2025

Automated Spatial QA/QC & CAD-to-GIS Migration Pipeline

A robust Python-based geospatial automation engine that extracts raw AutoCAD technical plans, audits topological connectivity, auto-corrects snapping errors, and writes validated features directly into PostGIS databases with automated compliance reports.
